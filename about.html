About Our Association

United by our commitment to justice, professional integrity and the advancement of the legal profession.

Our identity

A tradition of service and professional excellence.

The District Bar Association serves as a professional platform for advocates practising within the district. It seeks to encourage collegiality, ethical conduct and the continuous development of legal knowledge.

Through communication, professional activities and community engagement, the Association aims to contribute positively to the legal fraternity and the justice system.
